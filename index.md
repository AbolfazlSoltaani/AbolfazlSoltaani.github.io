## About Me

I have completed my B.Sc. in Computer Engineering from Sharif University of Technology and have been accepted to pursue a Ph.D. in the Theory Group at the Department of Computer Science, University of Toronto (deferred due to visa processing 😢). My research interests lie in combinatorics, graph theory, and algorithm design.

I have had the opportunity to work on various research projects, including parameterized algorithms based on control flow graphs under the supervision of Prof. Amir Kafshdar Goharshady. I also worked on convex optimization problems in sublinear settings with Prof. Andreas Karrenbauer and explored algorithms with predictions under the guidance of Golnoosh Shahkarami.

In addition to my research background, I have extensive software engineering experience. I worked for two years as a software engineer at [Sotoon](https://sotoon.ir), and since May 2025, I have been working as a software engineer at [Snapp!](https://snapp.ir), Iran's largest transportation company.

When I’m not immersed in research, I enjoy playing video games and staying active through sports.
